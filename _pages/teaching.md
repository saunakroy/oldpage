About nine years ago, a collaborative coding project in middle school began my fascination with the vast and expanding field of computer science. I was part of our school’s STEM club, and along with four other students, we decided to take on a unique challenge. We tried to create a virtual reality game about the solar system using the Unity game development platform. None of us had experience with Unity or programming in the C# language required for the project, but within all of us was a sense of curiosity and determination. We spent several months attempting to bring our idea to fruition, and had to resort to several places for assistance when we hit a roadblock. It was a tiresome process, but eventually we arrived at a complete, final product that made each of us extremely proud of our efforts. Several years later, I’ve maintained that same level of dedication and curiosity during my time as a computer science and mathematics student at the University of Maryland.

During my first two years as a student, I have taken numerous courses that have further piqued my interest in the field, including Discrete Mathematics, Machine Learning, and Algorithms. Some of these courses were quite grueling and math-intensive; however, to me, this made it all the more exhilarating. I have never stepped away from a challenge in these courses, and every mistake I made has been a source of inspiration and motivation to keep doing better. Outside the classroom, I’ve engaged in a variety of activities to further my knowledge of the fields of machine learning, computer vision, and data science, which are my primary interests. I’ve completed research projects on data science, analyzing undergraduate perceptions of AI, and developed tools for classifying brain tumor MRI scans from thousands of images. I’ve volunteered and mentored at hackathons like Bitcamp and Technica and taught students concepts in AI and machine learning through multiple student organizations. Each new learning initiative I took upon myself opened the doors to several others, and I’ve never backed down from a new, exciting opportunity to expand my skill set.

Looking ahead, I aim to further enrich my knowledge by pursuing graduate research in machine learning or computer vision, focusing on applications in the healthcare sector. I envision myself developing novel solutions to pressing medical challenges, some of which may involve disease diagnosis, personalized treatment planning, medical imagery, etc. I hope my portfolio demonstrates my keen interest in the intersection of these fields, and I plan on contributing more relevant projects, papers, and professional achievements as I boldly venture through my career!
